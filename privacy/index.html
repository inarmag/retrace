Privacy policy

Updated 21 October 2026

What stays on your phone

Your best score and your settings. There is no account, and we never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.

Analytics

The game sends anonymous gameplay events (sessions, the round a run ended, ads watched) to GameAnalytics under a random id made on your phone. Its SDK adds your device model, iOS version and the vendor identifier, plus the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking.

Ads

Ads are served by Google AdMob and only play when you choose to watch one. Where required by law you are asked for consent first, and iOS asks before any tracking. You can change your choice any time in the game's settings or in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Notifications

Reminders, if you turn them on, are created on your phone. Nothing is sent to a server.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Questions or deletion requests: velvet.osiris@gmail.com
